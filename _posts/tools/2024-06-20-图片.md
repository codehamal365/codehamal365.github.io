## Stock Photos and Images

- [免费素材图片](https://www.pexels.com/zh-cn/): High-quality free stock photos and videos, perfect for blogs, websites, and presentations.
- [Wallpapers - 图片资源](https://unsplash.dogedoge.com/t/wallpapers): Collection of beautiful wallpapers and background images from Unsplash.
- [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/): Free images, videos, and music with no attribution required for most uses.
- [Freepik](https://freepik.com/): Vast library of free and premium vectors, photos, and PSD files.

## Badges and Metadata

- [Shields.io: Quality metadata badges for open source projects](https://shields.io/): Generate dynamic badges for GitHub projects, showing build status, version, etc.

## Image Hosting

- [Postimages — 免费图片托管 / 图片上传](https://postimages.org/): Free image hosting with direct links and BBCode for forums.
- [Imgur](https://imgur.com/): Popular platform for uploading and sharing images with community features.

## Tips for Using Image Resources

- **Licensing**: Always check the license terms; many free resources require attribution or have restrictions on commercial use.
- **Optimization**: Use tools like TinyPNG for compressing images to improve website load times.
- **Badges**: Shields.io badges can be embedded in README files to display project status dynamically.
- **Hosting**: Choose reliable hosts for permanent links, especially for documentation or portfolios.
